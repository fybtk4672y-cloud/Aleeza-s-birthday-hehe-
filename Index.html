Dear Aleeza,

HAPPY BIRTHDAYYYYY. 💗 First of all, I hope you know how ridiculous it is that you get an entire website dedicated to you. Please appreciate my hard work. I'm practically the best person you know. You're welcome.

But seriously, I'm so glad we became friends. I don't even know how to explain it properly. You're one of those people I can talk to about literally anything. One minute we're discussing something normal, and the next we're making the most absurd jokes imaginable. Somehow, with you, it all makes sense.

I know you can seem cold or serious to people who don't know you, but I've seen how thoughtful you really are. I notice when you ask how I'm doing. I notice when you remember things I love. And because you're not always the most expressive person, those little moments when you tell me something means a lot to you? They mean a lot to me too.

One thing I really want you to know is that you're genuinely beautiful. Not just saying it because it's your birthday. I mean it. And by the way, if you ever need glasses again, please stop saying they don't suit you. They DO. You look good in them. End of discussion.

We're in different schools now, and I hate that we don't get all those ordinary little moments together anymore. I miss being able to talk to you whenever, to tease you, to hear your laugh, and to feel completely comfortable being myself around you. Sometimes I miss our old conversations so much that I wish I could jump right back into one.

But I don't want us to become people who only remember that they used to be close. I want more stories, more random conversations, more outings, more laughing at things that wouldn't make sense to anyone else. Even if life gets busy and takes us in different directions, I hope we always make room for each other.

I pray Allah keeps you safe, healthy, happy and surrounded by people who genuinely care about you. May He protect you from things that hurt you, make your exams and your future easier, and help you become the person you want to be. You deserve to choose your own path, and I hope you never change yourself just to please someone who doesn't understand you.

And please, don't forget me. I'm joking. Mostly. I hope that years from now, when you think of school, I'm somewhere in those memories making you laugh. Because I know you'll always be in mine.

Thank you for being my friend. Thank you for being you. Happy birthday, Aleeza. I love you lots, and I hope this year is so, so good to you. 💗
